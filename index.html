Corporate Identity & Proof

Established in 2020, Techno Electric & Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd. (TEEC) operates with a singular focus: delivering technically sound, premium electrical and low-voltage systems for high-value properties.

Through resilient operations and a commitment to engineering standards, TEEC has successfully achieved a sustainable project turnover exceeding NPR 3.5 Crore, maintaining rigorous execution protocols across all active sites.
