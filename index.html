Founded in 2020, Techno Electric & Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd. (TEEC) was established to address a specific gap in the regional market: the requirement for rigorous, standards-compliant electrical contracting tailored to high-value real estate.

Navigating early global disruptions, TEEC has restructured and stabilized into a robust engineering entity. Our focus remains exclusively on executing precise electrical and ELV architectures, utilizing digital workflows and systematic project coordination to eliminate on-site ambiguity.

Core Execution Capabilities

Electrical Infrastructure

High and low tension installations, custom distribution panels, and comprehensive power routing engineered for load stability in commercial environments.

ELV & Data Networks

Structured cabling systems, secure network backbones, and precise wire mapping for unified communications, CCTV, and Public Address (PA) hardware.

Fire Detection Systems

Code-compliant layout, wiring, and integration of life-safety fire alarm networks tailored for high-occupancy hospitality structures.

Automation Readiness

Systems thinking applied to infrastructure routing, ensuring seamless future integration of IoT, BMS, and third-party hardware controllers.

Project Coordination

Rigorous on-site synchronization, digital blueprint adherence, and phased delivery protocols to align with broader construction timelines.

Integrated MEP Delivery Model

While our internal specialization dictates electrical and low-voltage frameworks, TEEC facilitates comprehensive mechanical requirements. We actively coordinate with specialized partners to deploy HVAC, Plumbing, and Fire Sprinkler systems under a unified turnkey project structure when required by client scope.
